Verification cadence

A status changes only when its dated record changes.

Verification records are classified as Documented cart result or Documented manual observation. A result is published only when the required cart fields and screenshot evidence are retained. Incomplete observations do not create a new verification date or a verified-at-checkout result.

The observation is limited to the visible guest-cart response. It does not establish a purchase, payment, shipping outcome, product quality, or a permanent merchant policy.
