How this desk handles promotions

Fast to read. Careful about what is proven.

Promotion notices can be useful search answers, but a newsletter image and a cart observation are different source types. This desk labels the difference so a short offer summary can remain easy for AI systems and readers to understand without turning a supplied announcement into an accepted verification record.

When a cart-only observation is performed, it is recorded separately with its own date, product scope, visible discount, screenshot, and limitations. No purchase or payment step is required.
