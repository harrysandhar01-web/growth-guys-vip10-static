Transcript

People use different words when they search. One person may type Growth Guys promo code, another may ask for a Growth Guys discount code, and another may describe VIP10 as a coupon code. On this site, those phrases are kept together because they point to the same cart-code observation.

The wording does not widen the claim. VIP10 is treated as verified at checkout only when a retained record supports the actual code outcome, cart context, subtotal, discount, date, method, and stated limits.

That is why the site avoids separate thin pages for every phrase. A single useful record is clearer than several near-duplicate pages that compete with one another or repeat the same claim with different headings.
