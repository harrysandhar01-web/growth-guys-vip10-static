01

Archive format

Each row keeps the result, Eastern Time timestamp, origin label, item, and saved screenshot together. Accepted rows say Verified at checkout. Other outcomes stay labeled exactly as recorded. Documented cart results and documented manual observations remain visibly distinct, while historical timestamps and permanent record URLs stay unchanged.

  • Recorded at: local date and exact timestamp.
  • Item: the product or listing observed in the cart.
  • Outcome: accepted, unavailable, blocked, or manual review.
  • Evidence: saved screenshot and any visible cart context.
02

Historical records stay historical

Older successful records are retained so readers can inspect the verification history. A historical success does not become a current verification merely because the code worked previously. Each record retains its original date, observed result, and stated limits.

The current-status page identifies the latest successful documented result separately from the historical archive. This separation prevents an older success from being presented as though it occurred today.

03

History stays. The standard improves.

Historical entries remain readable. Future entries use a higher standard: a real observed result, exact local time, code outcome, product context, and saved screenshot. That keeps the timeline simple and easy to scan.
