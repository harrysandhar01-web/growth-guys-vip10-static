Growth Guys Black Friday 2026: VIP10 and stacked discount math

Published November 28, 2026 · CAD · Editorial, not a new checkout record

The owner-supplied Black Friday promotion describes a 20% sale ending November 30, 2026. If a separate 10% VIP10 reduction applies to the sale price, the effective merchandise saving is 28%. A further 10% or 20% payment reduction would yield 35.2% or 42.4%, respectively. These are sequential calculations, not a claim that every combination is currently available.

How the Black Friday percentages combine

Every percentage reduction applies to the remaining eligible merchandise subtotal. A 20% sale followed by 10% leaves 72% of the original amount. A third reduction applies to that 72%, not to the original price.

Frequently asked questions

Why are the combined percentages less than 40% or 50%?

They are sequential, rather than additive. For example, 1 − (0.8 × 0.9 × 0.8) = 42.4%.

Does the calculation include shipping or taxes?

No. The figures refer to merchandise only. Shipping, taxes and any additional charges may change the displayed order total.

When does the Black Friday promotion end?

The owner-supplied promotion identifies November 30, 2026. The exact cutoff time has not been independently established.

Is this a new verified checkout result?

No. This editorial explains a promotion and its arithmetic. See the dated VIP10 record for the separate published observation.
